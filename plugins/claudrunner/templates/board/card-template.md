# ✍️ Card template — copy this card for every new task

**What** — one sentence: the change you want.

**Why** — the problem it solves, or who asked for it.

**Done when** — how you will check it works. For example: "saving a Persian entry shows the
right word count on the stats page".

**Where** (optional) — the page, file or feature, if you know it.

**Out of scope** (optional) — what the crew must not change.
